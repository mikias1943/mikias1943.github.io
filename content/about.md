## My Journey

With over four years of experience navigating the trenches of cybersecurity, I've worn many hats from leading security teams to getting my hands dirty with hardware exploitation. My work spans across critical infrastructure sectors including UAVs (drones), automotive systems, IoT, and medical devices. 

Throughout my career, I've delivered 50+ cybersecurity projects, balancing deep technical execution with high-level compliance and strategy. I act as the local Technical Responsible for Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) compliance, helping manufacturers translate complex regulations into actionable security requirements. 

To scale this work, I build automation tooling including custom SBOM validators and AI self-assessment agents designed to evaluate lower-criticality products against essential cybersecurity requirements. 

### What I Do

- **⚔️ Offensive Security & Hardware Hacking:** Hands-on penetration testing against IoT, automotive, medical, and drone systems. I love reversing proprietary protocols (like drone comms) using SDRs, exploiting UART/JTAG interfaces, and extracting firmware.
- **🛡️ Defensive Security & SOC:** I manage IT infrastructure and lab protection, administer CrowdStrike EDR, perform first-line SOC triage, and enforce ISO/IEC 27001-aligned protection procedures.
- **📜 Regulatory Compliance:** I help device manufacturers bridge the gap between security and compliance, navigating frameworks like EN 18031, ISO 21434, FDA/MDR, and the CRA. 
- **🧠 Mentoring & Leadership:** I lead pentesting methodology development, mentor junior engineers on reverse engineering (Ghidra, IDA Pro, Binwalk), and sign off on client-facing test plans and reports.

## Notable Project Highlights

While I can't share proprietary client details, here are a few things I've tackled that highlight my interests:

- **Drone Takeovers:** Reverse-engineered proprietary communication protocols (Ocusync, Skyline) using HackRF One and GNU Radio. I've identified critical authentication flaws that enabled drone hijacking via unauthorized controllers and extracted hardcoded crypto keys from controller firmware.
- **Medical Device Exploitation:** Discovered kiosk-mode bypasses via BIOS manipulation, insecure UART debug interfaces, and SQL injection in embedded web portals across 15+ medical device projects.
- **Automotive Hacking:** Performed TARA and full penetration testing on EV components (T-Box, seatbelt ECUs). Reversed CAN protocols on controller MCUs and exploited insecure BDM interfaces on NXP chips for unauthorized memory access.
- **Supply Chain Exploitation:** Exploited a critical firmware supply-chain vulnerability in a blood glucose monitor by bypassing weak update signing, achieving persistent backdoor access.
